Starting

A short note on why this site exists and what I'm planning to do with it.

This is the first post. It exists mostly to prove the pipes work, but also to set out what I want this space to be.

I've spent five years in IT, mostly around Microsoft 365 and cybersecurity, working at a managed service provider. A lot of what I've learned sits in my head or in scattered notes, and I wanted somewhere to think it through properly, in public, where being wrong is more useful.

The plan is to write about the work as it actually is: migrations, identity, the operational side of running an MSP, and the commercial questions underneath all of it. Less vendor gloss, more view from the floor.

More soon.

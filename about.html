About

A bit about me, and the things that hold my attention.

Who I am

I'm Josh, an IT Systems Engineer based in London. I've been working for five years now, mostly around Microsoft 365 and cybersecurity services. I work at a managed service provider, where the days are split between tenant administration, endpoint management, client communications, and trying to make IT actually work for small businesses without overpromising.

Outside the technical side, I read widely and think too much about how this work is run and where it's heading. This site exists because I wanted somewhere to write that down.

What I do

Most of what I work on falls under one of a few headings: keeping Microsoft 365 tenants healthy and secure; running migrations between them when companies merge, split, or change identity stacks; and figuring out which automation and AI tooling is genuinely worth deploying versus the noise.

I'm a practitioner more than a strategist. I write from inside the work, not above it, and that tends to be where the useful detail lives.

What I write about

A few rough buckets. The MSP view from the floor: what running IT for small businesses actually looks like, including the unglamorous bits. Microsoft 365 in practice: migrations, identity, Conditional Access, Intune, and the gap between vendor documentation and reality. And the commercial side of MSPs: how they price, position, and compete in the market as it stands.

Occasionally something further afield: a book, a tool, a question about how this work fits into the wider direction technology is heading.

Get in touch

You can reach me at hello@morrisjb.com, or find me on LinkedIn. If you're working on something interesting in the MSP space or in applied AI for IT, I'd genuinely like to hear from you.

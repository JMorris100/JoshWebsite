About

A bit about me, and the things that hold my attention.

Who I am

I'm Josh — a systems engineer based in London. I work at a managed service provider, where my days are split between Microsoft 365 administration, endpoint management, client communications, and trying to make IT actually work for small businesses without overpromising.

Outside work, I read widely and think too much about how technology shapes the way we work and live. This site exists because I wanted somewhere to write that down.

What I do

Most of what I work on falls under one of three headings: keeping Microsoft 365 tenants healthy and secure; running migrations between them when companies merge, split, or change identity stacks; and figuring out which automation and AI tooling is genuinely worth deploying versus the noise.

I'm a practitioner more than a strategist — I write from inside the work, not above it. That tends to be where the useful detail lives.

What I write about

Three rough buckets:

  • The MSP view from the floor — what running IT for small businesses actually looks like, including the unglamorous bits.
  • Microsoft 365 in practice — migrations, identity, Conditional Access, Intune, and the gap between vendor documentation and reality.
  • AI in IT services — what works, what doesn't, and why the honest answer is usually narrower than the marketing.

Occasionally something further afield — a book, a tool, a question about how this work fits into the wider direction technology is heading.

Get in touch

You can reach me at hello@morrisjb.com, or find me on LinkedIn. If you're working on something interesting in the MSP space or in applied AI for IT, I'd genuinely like to hear from you.
